Privacy Policy for Andoria

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Andoria is a video editing application that allows users to edit, enhance, export, and share their own video content.

Information We Access

Andoria may access videos, audio, and other media selected by the user in order to provide editing and export features. This media is processed for the features requested by the user.

TikTok Integration

If a user chooses to connect Andoria with TikTok, Andoria may request authorization necessary to upload user-selected video content to TikTok. Andoria will only perform these actions when initiated by the user.

TikTok account information and authorization are handled according to TikTok's applicable authentication and API requirements.

Sharing Content

Andoria allows users to choose when and where their exported content is shared. Andoria does not publish videos to social platforms without an action initiated by the user.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by Andoria and limit access to what is necessary to provide the requested functionality.

Third-Party Services

Andoria may interact with third-party services such as TikTok when explicitly requested by the user. Use of those services is also subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Andoria develops or its features change. Updates will be published on this page.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Andoria developer through the official Andoria project repository.
